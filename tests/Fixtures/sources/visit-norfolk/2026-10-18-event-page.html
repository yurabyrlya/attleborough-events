            About Parkrun

            Ready, set. . .parkrun! Start your weekend with a free, fun, and friendly weekly 5k community event. Walk, jog, run, volunteer or spectate – it’s up to you!

            The course

            If you’re looking for a single loop route with a few hills, tarmac and hardcore roads and unbeatable views – this is the parkrun for you.

            Take your marks at the start line next to the South front of Holkham Hall and set off up the gentle incline and head through the gate on the right side of the cattle grid. Make your way round the left-hand side of  the ‘Rob-elisk’ where volunteer Rob and his four legged friends will be waiting to cheer you on!

            At the bottom of the hill, take a left up the hill towards the Great Barn and then head back down the slope on the right. When you get to the avenue turn right and head all they way down the south drive, back up the hill to the Obelisk (passing on the left) and then it’s all downhill from there to the finish on a short stretch of grass in front of the Hall.

            Collect your token, scan your barcode and (if you feel like it) ring the happy bell to celebrate a wonderful parkrun!
