What's On

Live your best life in Wymondham. Soak up culture from art exhibitions, classical concerts and other live music to stand-up comedy, poetry jams, book readings, and vibrant fairs and festivals throughout the year.

There’s fun for all the family, all ages and abilities, from baby bounce and rhyme at the Wymondham Library, to aerobics for seniors. Stay fit and healthy with sports clubs offering activities from archery to yoga. And our modern leisure centre has a swimming pool, gym and spa to help you unwind.

Wymondham’s community spaces and venues host a wide range of activities, clubs and social groups, providing plenty of opportunities to meet new people, learn new skills and get involved in local life. Whether you’re interested in wellbeing, crafts, music, dance or community projects, there’s something to suit every interest.

The town also comes alive with seasonal events throughout the year. From bustling markets and family fun days to heritage celebrations, food and drink events and Christmas traditions, there’s always something happening. Whether you’re visiting for the day or looking to become part of the community, Wymondham offers plenty of ways to connect, take part and make lasting memories.
