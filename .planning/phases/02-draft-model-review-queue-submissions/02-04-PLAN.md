<objective>
Carry the organiser's contact choice from entry to the public site. The `events` table gains `contact_public` and `organiser_website` (D-19); the admin form, `PublishableEventData` (including `fromDraft()`), the event page and the calendar card honour them. Public submissions (02-05) default `contact_public` to false, so this plan is what keeps a private email private once a submission is approved.

Purpose: the privacy half of SUB-01's contact fields (D-03, D-19). The interim /privacy page that promises this is in 02-05.
Output: one migration, model/DTO/form changes, read-side gating, and tests.
</objective>

Supports success criteria 1 and 3: organisers' details are handled as promised when their event is approved.
