<objective>
Score every draft deterministically and suggest venues. `QualityScorer` turns spec §8.2 factors into a 0-100 score, band and plain-English reasons; `VenueMatcher` finds the likely venue (exact, alias, postcode, then trigram) without ever creating one; `DraftWriter` re-scores on every write.

Purpose: PIPE-04; the venue half of D-05 (the review page and edit page use `suggest()` in 02-12/02-14); feeds bulk approve's Low-quality skip (02-03 keys).
Output: two services, two value objects, the DraftWriter hook, and three tests.
</objective>
