<objective>
Make the running system observable by its single admin (OPS-04). The scheduler and the queue worker each prove they are alive by writing a heartbeat row; the daily occurrence sync records its result; a Filament "System health" page shows how fresh each one is against stale thresholds, lists failed jobs and lets the admin retry or delete them. Laravel logs move to a daily channel kept 14 days, and the admin panel stops loading its font from a third-party host. Everything is proven locally with the real long-running processes (`php artisan schedule:work`, `php artisan queue:work`): heartbeats fresh, and a deliberately failed job that shows up and is then retried successfully. The production half (shared/.env log values, the systemd worker proof, ops/ops-drill.sh, HEALTHY within 7 minutes of a deploy, ops/check.sh and ops/README.md edits) is deferred to Phase 5 plan 05-02 by the owner's 2026-10-16 scope change (Phase 1 is local-dev + CI only).

Plan 01-04 added the 03:10 sync; the production box with journald/Caddy log caps, the systemd worker and cron arrives in Phase 5 (05-01). Without this plan a dead cron or worker would go unnoticed (PITFALLS Pitfall 10: "nothing alerts because the thing that would alert is the scheduler").
Output: heartbeats table and model, QueueHeartbeat and OpsDrillJob, HealthReport, SystemHealth page, ops:health and ops:drill commands, schedule entries, daily-log config in config/logging.php and .env.example, and the local admin font.
</objective>
