## Third-party host deny-list

Use this regex wherever a task says "the deny-list" (CI gate, live check, tests):
`fonts\.bunny\.net|fonts\.googleapis\.com|fonts\.gstatic\.com|use\.typekit\.net|cdn\.jsdelivr\.net|unpkg\.com|cdnjs\.cloudflare\.com|cdn\.tailwindcss\.com`
The deny-list only catches regressions to known CDNs. The real guarantee is the allow-list rule: resource URLs must be relative or on the host of `config('app.url')` (local APP_URL now; `whatsonattleborough.co.uk` in production from Phase 5).
