Visitors filter by Today / This weekend / This week (Europe/London overlap semantics per CAL-02, correct across the 25 Oct 2026 and 28 Mar 2027 clock changes, multi-day events in every overlapping window), by category, to Free events and to family-friendly events. </objective>
