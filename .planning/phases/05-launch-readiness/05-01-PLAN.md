Get the production box live: the owner registers the domain, puts DNS and Email Routing on Cloudflare and creates the Hetzner server, choosing the OS image at a decision checkpoint. Then a versioned provisioning script turns the bare VPS into PHP 8.5-FPM + PostgreSQL 18 + Caddy, serving a PHP probe over HTTPS at `https://whatsonattleborough.co.uk` with www→apex 301. Deployer's provision recipe is not used (RESEARCH Summary 5b).
